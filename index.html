Frequently asked questions

How long does customs clearance take?

It depends on how complete your documents are, how the Bureau of Customs assesses the shipment, and whether it is selected for examination. Complete, correct documents make the biggest difference, so we check them before filing. Send us your shipment details and we will give you a realistic estimate.

What do I need for AMO-BOC accreditation?

The Account Management Office (AMO) of the Bureau of Customs accredits importers and exporters. Requirements are updated from time to time, so we confirm the current list for your type of business, then prepare and process the application for you.

What happens if my cargo is selected for examination?

The Bureau of Customs may ask for a physical inspection of your cargo. Our team coordinates the examination, keeps you updated and follows up on the result so the shipment can move toward release.

Who pays the duties and taxes?

The importer pays, based on the assessed value and the tariff classification of the goods. We confirm the amounts with you before payment so there are no surprises.

Does my cargo need a permit?

Regulated goods such as food, medicines and chemicals may need permits from other government agencies before customs can release them. Tell us what you are shipping and we will advise what applies.

Terms worth knowing

AWBAir waybill

The transport document for air cargo, issued by the airline or forwarder.

B/LBill of lading

The transport document for sea cargo, issued by the shipping line. It proves ownership of the goods.

BOCBureau of Customs

The government agency that assesses duties and taxes and releases imported cargo.

AMOAccount Management Office

The BOC office that handles accreditation of importers, exporters and brokers.

BreakbulkNon-containerised cargo

Loose or oversized goods shipped individually rather than inside a container.

Customs declarantDeclaration preparer

The person who prepares and files the customs declaration for your shipment.

Commercial invoiceSeller's bill

Lists the goods, quantities and prices. Customs uses it to assess duties and taxes.

DemurrageContainer storage charge

A fee from the shipping line when a container stays at the port past the free time.
